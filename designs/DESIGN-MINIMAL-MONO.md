Black, white, and exactly one accent color. No ornament - every element earns its place.

Palette: bg #ffffff, surface #fafafa, text #111111, muted #737373, accent #2563eb (swap to match the request's product), border #e5e5e5
Type: one geometric sans (Inter/"Helvetica Neue"/system-ui), weights 400/600 only. Display 52px/600/-1.5px, H2 30px/600, body 16px/400/1.55.
Spacing/radius: 8/16/24/48/80px scale; radius 8/12px only - no pills, no sharp corners
Shadow: none by default; one subtle border-only card treatment (1px solid border, no shadow)

Do: lots of negative space; a single strong accent color used sparingly (CTA buttons/links only); left-align most text
Don't: use gradients; use more than one accent color; add decorative icons/illustrations unless the request asks; use shadows for elevation
