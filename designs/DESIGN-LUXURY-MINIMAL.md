Quiet luxury - black, white, one metallic accent. Confidence through restraint and generous space, not decoration.

Palette: bg #0f0f0f (dark) or #ffffff (light - pick one per request), text is the inverse of bg, muted #9a9a94, accent #b8985a (muted gold), border rgba(255,255,255,0.08) or rgba(0,0,0,0.08) to match
Type: a refined serif (Cormorant/Playfair Display) for headlines at wide letter-spacing, sans (system-ui) for body, weight 400 only plus one 500 for emphasis. Display 42px/400/1px letter-spacing (uppercase eyebrows), body 16px/400/1.7.
Spacing/radius: 16/32/56/96/160px scale (unusually spacious); radius 0/2px only - no rounding
Shadow: none - separation via space and thin gold-tinted 1px borders only

Do: full-bleed imagery; centered, symmetric layouts; uppercase letter-spaced eyebrow labels above headings; lots of empty space
Don't: use rounded corners or pill buttons; use more than one accent color; crowd content; use a sans headline font
