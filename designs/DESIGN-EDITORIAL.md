Warm, literary, magazine-like. Generous whitespace, serif headlines, a cream canvas instead of stark white - feels written, not engineered.

Palette: bg #f7f4ed, surface #fcfbf8, text #1c1c1c, muted #6b6558, accent #b5482a (terracotta), border #e8e3d8
Type: headlines in a serif (Georgia/"Iowan Old Style"/"Source Serif 4"), body in a clean sans (system-ui); weights 400/600 only. Display 56px/600/-1px, H2 34px/600, body 17px/400/1.6 line-height.
Spacing/radius: 8/16/24/40/64/96px scale; radius 4/8/16px (no full pills)
Shadow: one soft ambient shadow only - 0 2px 12px rgba(28,28,28,0.06)

Do: pull-quotes in the accent color; wide line-length caps (~65ch); an understated top nav (just text links); real breathing room between sections
Don't: use pure white backgrounds; use more than 2 font weights; use saturated/neon colors; crowd sections together
