Trustworthy and buttoned-up. White space, navy accents, restrained type - reads as competent, not flashy.

Palette: bg #ffffff, surface #f5f7fa, text #1a2233, muted #5b6472, accent #1e3a8a (navy), accent2 #2563eb (brighter, for links/CTAs), border #e2e6ec
Type: sans-serif (system-ui/"Helvetica Neue"), weights 400/600/700 (this system allows 700 for emphasis, unlike most others in this pool). Display 44px/700, H2 28px/600, body 16px/400/1.6.
Spacing/radius: 8/16/24/40/64px scale; radius 4/8px only - crisp, not soft
Shadow: minimal - 0 1px 3px rgba(26,34,51,0.08) on cards; borders do most of the separation

Do: a clear top nav with a solid CTA button; stat/logo bars for credibility; structured 2/3-column grid layouts
Don't: use pill-radius buttons; use bright/saturated non-navy accents; use playful or handwritten type; leave sections asymmetric
